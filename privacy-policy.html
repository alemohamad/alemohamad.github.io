Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the apps I make and publish on the App Store, Google Play and the Meta Horizon Store as Ale Mohamad: Burin, Dexa, Plyra, Sūji, Fuengirola Bus, Nawal, Gentleman, Clina, Simple Coffee Ratio and Numo.

The short version

My apps don't collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you create in an app stays on your device, or in your own cloud account, such as iCloud, if the app offers it and you choose to use it. I never see it.

What I don't do

  • I don't collect personal information such as your name, email address, location or contacts.
  • I don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • I don't sell, rent or share any information with anyone.
  • I don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information handled by the stores

Some things are handled by the store you download an app from, not by me, and are covered by that store's privacy policy: Apple, Google or Meta.

  • Purchases and downloads go through the App Store, Google Play or the Meta Horizon Store. I never receive your payment details or account information.
  • iCloud: on Apple devices, when an app saves your files or data to iCloud, they're stored in your own iCloud account. I have no access to them.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: depending on your device settings, the store may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with me. They don't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in your device's settings. On Apple devices, for example, it's "Share with App Developers" in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Permissions

If an app asks for a permission, it explains why when it asks. Anything it gets access to is used only on your device, for that feature, and is never sent to me. You can say no, or change your mind later in your device's settings; only the feature that needs it will stop working.

Links to other websites

Some apps may link to websites I don't run. Their own privacy policies apply there, and I'm not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

My apps don't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If an app ever starts handling your data differently, I'll say so here before that version is released.
